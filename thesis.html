Dose- and Time-Dependent Biphasic Modulation of Physiological Responses by Poppy Seed Supplementation in Rabbits

MPhil in Zoology · University of Gujrat · In vivo (Rabbit) Model

Abstract

This thesis investigates how graded doses of poppy seed supplementation influence physiological responses in a rabbit model over time. Using a controlled in vivo design, the work characterises biphasic, dose- and time-dependent shifts across haematological, biochemical, and tissue-level parameters, clarifying the points at which supplementation supports normal physiology and the points at which it begins to strain it.

Objectives

To evaluate the dose-dependent effects of poppy seed supplementation on growth and haematological indices; to assess biochemical and redox markers of tissue function; and to identify the time windows at which responses transition between beneficial and adverse — the biphasic effect.
